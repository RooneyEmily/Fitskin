\begin{abstract}
We evaluate a chart-free iPhone ProRAW flash/no-flash pipeline for recovering
\textbf{forehead} CIELAB under CIE D65 against FitSkin Inside Lab forehead
references, when subjects are illuminated by a controlled \textbf{Variable
Lighting Ring Light} at two ring settings (daylight-like \textbf{D65} and warm
fluorescent-like \textbf{F12}).
Captures are torch zips (no-flash + flash DNGs + Apple Face landmarks) across
four participants with a camera white-balance factorial (cells A--E).
The deployment stack extends \pipename{} with (i)~Bradford chromatic adaptation
in \texttt{hybrid\_deploy} mode (Lu ambient CCT on warm/F12 scenes; frozen
5500\,K on D65-like scenes), (ii)~an illuminant-routed multi-Lab affine
corrector, and (iii)~FairFace-7-routed \texttt{specular\_tone} sampling on the
forehead ROI, with cheek pooling when forehead $L^*$ is spatially uniform.

On the full evaluable cohort ($N=84$ trials; forehead trimmed-mean Lab for
cohort tables), mean $\deee$ vs FitSkin forehead falls from
\num{16.49} (frozen 5500\,K) to \num{11.95} with hybrid + multi-Lab
(\num{9.92} on D65 ring, \num{14.18} on F12 ring).
To isolate capture WB from person effects without confounding, Colab runs a
\textbf{within-subject WB sweep} on \textbf{Parker} (D65 cells A--E and F12
cells B--E; no F12-A in the cohort) under the production specular-tone path.
Parker achieves mean $\deee=\num{3.5}$ on D65 and \num{1.6} on F12 with
forehead $L^*$ within $\sim$3 units of FitSkin reference.
In-situ ring measurements confirm D65 $\approx$6700\,K and F12 $\approx$3000\,K;
residual D65 error on other participants (e.g.\ Lihn with $L^*\gtrsim 65$) is
dominated by capture exposure, not illuminant mis-labeling.
Capture WB is recorded in filenames / Booth design, but the color path demosaics
with \textbf{pre-AWB} linear RAW and does not apply phone as-shot white balance.
\end{abstract}

\section{Motivation and goals}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Problem}
Indoor chart-free colorimetry on Pansor-20 (fixed room light) reaches mean
$\deee\approx\num{3.6}$ with \pipename{} at a fixed 5500\,K chromatic adaptation
(CAT) white~\cite{placeholder-d65-roi}.
Under a \emph{variable} ring light, the same frozen CAT is expected to fail on
warm F12: cheek/forehead chroma shifts and $\deee$ rises into the mid-teens.
We need a claimable illuminant-aware path that:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Matches the FitSkin \textbf{forehead} measurement site (not only cheek);
  \item Uses the known iPhone torch SPD as a flash prior for Lu ambient CCT;
  \item Remains chart-free at inference (ColorChecker may appear in frame for
        diagnostics but is not required);
  \item Documents sensitivity to the Booth capture white-balance factorial.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Contributions (this report)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item Full description of the Variable Lighting Ring Light experiment
        (participants, ring illuminants, WB cells, torch-zip protocol).
  \item Production stack: forehead ROI + \texttt{hybrid\_deploy} CAT +
        multi-illuminant Lab affine + FairFace-7 \texttt{specular\_tone}
        (with forehead$\cup$cheek pooling when $L^*$ is uniform).
  \item Cohort results ($N=84$) with $\deee$ grouped by capture \texttt{wb\_cell}.
  \item Within-subject WB factorial on \textbf{Parker} (9 zips; production
        specular-tone path) with illumination and segmentation visualizations
        in Colab.
  \item Colab notebook: Drive-backed Parker WB sweep, pinned $N=84$ tables,
        D65/F12 face-crop grid, and ROI overlay diagnostics
        (\texttt{ringlight\_best\_stack\_inference\_colab.ipynb}).
\end{itemize}

\section{Experimental setup}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Booth and ring light}
Subjects sit facing a Variable Lighting Ring Light in a controlled booth.
Two ring programs are used:
\begin{description}[leftmargin=*,style=nextline]
  \item[D65 ring] Daylight-like ambient; design matched white balance near
    6500\,K (cell~C). Handheld spectrometer readings under the ring report
    CCT $\approx\num{6704}$\,K ($x=0.310$, $y=0.324$; blue-pump LED SPD with
    peak $\lambda_p\approx 444$\,nm).
  \item[F12 ring] Warm fluorescent-like ambient; design matched white balance
    near 3000\,K (cell~C). Measured CCT $\approx\num{3037}$\,K ($x=0.428$,
    $y=0.391$; broad warm peak $\lambda_p\approx 607$\,nm). This is the stress
    condition for frozen 5500\,K CAT.
\end{description}

\subsection{Capture protocol: torch zips}
Each trial is a Pansor ``Torch'' zip containing:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{No-flash} ProRAW DNG ($A_0$);
  \item \textbf{Flash} ProRAW DNG ($B_0$, iPhone torch);
  \item Apple Vision \textbf{face landmarks} JSON (cheek / forehead regions).
\end{itemize}
Demosaic uses \texttt{use\_camera\_wb=False} (unity / pre-AWB linear RGB) so
that Bradford CAT, not the phone's as-shot multipliers, defines the scene white.

of five design cells (same design for all participants; values from Booth

ColorChecker-detectable frames in the ring CC manifest

\section{Color pipeline}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Frozen colorimetric path}
For each zip we form geometric-mean reflectance
$R_0 = \sqrt{\max(A_0,0)\odot \max(B_0',0)}$,
map camera RGB to XYZ via \texttt{tier3\_affine}, apply Bradford CAT to D65,
and pool Lab on the ROI.

\subsection{Illuminant-aware CAT: \texttt{hybrid\_deploy}}
Lu \& Drew (2006) log-chroma residual estimates ambient CCT from the flash pair,
using the measured torch SPD as flash prior.
\texttt{hybrid\_deploy} uses Lu CCT on F12/warm scenes and frozen 5500\,K on
D65-like scenes (Lu alone is biased cold on D65).

\subsection{Multi-illuminant Lab affine}
Illuminant-routed $3{\times}4$ Lab affine (\texttt{multi\_illuminant\_lab\_affine.json})
with separate $W_{\mathrm{d65}}$ / $W_{\mathrm{f12}}$ weights.

\subsection{ROI: forehead matching FitSkin}
\label{sec:roi}
Apple Vision landmarks define a forehead mask (with hair refinement via
\texttt{refine\_forehead\_mask}).
Colab Cell~3 overlays:

  \item \textbf{Green} --- forehead ROI (FitSkin scan site);
  \item \textbf{Blue} --- cheek band (for context);
  \item \textbf{Yellow} --- Lab pool (forehead$\cup$cheek) when
        \texttt{specular\_tone} expands sampling on F12.

\subsection{FairFace-7 and \texttt{specular\_tone}}
\label{sec:specular}
FairFace-7 routes specular/shadow $L^*$ sampling.
When forehead $L^*$ is uniform (std $\lesssim 2.5$) on F12 or for Indian
ethnicity, we pool forehead$\cup$cheek pixels.
On Parker F12 all four cells expand the pool; on Parker D65 the forehead alone
has enough $L^*$ variation and pooling stays off.

\section{Discussion}
%=============================================================================

\paragraph{Ring illuminants are correct.}
Spectrometer readings ($\sim$6700\,K D65, $\sim$3000\,K F12) match the Booth
design. High $\deee$ on \emph{some} D65 captures is not explained by treating
the ring as the wrong illuminant.

\paragraph{Exposure dominates person-specific D65 error.}
On Lihn (not used as the Colab demo), D65 production-path $\deee$ reaches
\num{9}--\num{17} because forehead $L^*\approx 65$--\num{75} vs FitSkin
\num{55.2}---often flagged \texttt{L\_ge\_75} / out-of-band---while F12 on the
same subject stays $\deee\approx 1$--\num{4} with cheek pool engaged.
Parker illustrates the intended operating point: $L^*$ near reference and
$\deee<5$ on D65, $<2.5$ on F12.

\paragraph{Site match and warm light.}
Forehead ROI alignment and hybrid + multi-Lab affine recover most of the F12
cohort gap ($N=84$ F12 mean \num{14.18} vs D65 \num{9.92}).
Specular\_tone + cheek pool remains essential for uniform foreheads (Anjana).

\paragraph{Capture WB factorial.}
Cross-cohort ($N=84$) and within-subject (Parker) views both show non-monotonic
cell effects, consistent with pre-AWB demosaic: phone WB is logged but not the
CAT white.

\paragraph{Colab reproducibility.}
Setup extracts \texttt{colab\_assets/ringlight\_best\_stack.zip} (includes
\texttt{wb\_sweep\_parker.json} and \texttt{eval\_n84\_by\_wb\_cell.json}).
Cell~2b resolves Parker zips from Drive; Cell~3 exports tables and figures;
Cell~6 loads pinned $N=84$ tables (inline fallback if git lags).

\section{Conclusions}
%=============================================================================

Under Variable Lighting Ring Light (D65 / F12), a chart-free forehead stack
combining \texttt{hybrid\_deploy} CAT, multi-illuminant Lab affine, and
FairFace-routed specular sampling improves frozen 5500\,K CAT
($N=84$: $\deee$ \num{16.49}$\,\to\,$\num{11.95}).
A Parker within-subject WB sweep demonstrates production accuracy
($\deee\approx\num{3.5}$ D65, $\approx\num{1.6}$ F12) when captures are
well exposed.
Ring CCT measurements validate the illuminant labels; residual error is driven
by forehead $L^*$ exposure and person-specific tonality, not phone WB in the
CAT path.
