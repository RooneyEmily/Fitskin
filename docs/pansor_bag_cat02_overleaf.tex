\section{Methods}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Cohort and reference standard}

We evaluated paired iPhone ProRAW (DNG) flash/no-flash captures from the \emph{Pansor} cohort (16~June~2026): two participants $\times$ two in-scene conditions (Sephora Bag, ColorChecker) $\times$ three trials ($N=12$ pairs). Ground truth was FitSkin multi-point \emph{cheek} CIELAB under D65, taken as the May~20 median cheek Lab per participant (cross-session reference; capture $\approx$4 weeks before the iPhone session). Agreement was quantified as CIEDE2000 ($\deee$) between pipeline cheek means and FitSkin cheek means. This cohort is \emph{not} same-session validation; absolute $\deee$ is expected to exceed the booth Phase~4 evaluation (median $\deee=3.50$, $N=5$, same-session mapping).

\subsection{RAW ingestion and offline calibration}

DNGs were demosaiced to linear camera RGB (\texttt{rawpy}, half-resolution demosaic). App-exported ProRAW required embedded camera white balance; unity white balance caused severe chroma failure on this data. A per-channel 99.5th-percentile scale mapped linear RGB to $[0,1]$ for alignment and masking; reported statistics used linear values before display encoding.

Offline calibration used separate training captures with a visible 24-patch Macbeth ColorChecker. A production $3\times4$ affine map $\mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{aff}}$ from $[R,G,B,1]^\top$ to CIE~XYZ (D65) was fit by stacked least squares with anchor down-weighting. Per-participant exposure anchors (median white-patch scale from training) were applied to reflectance before XYZ$\rightarrow$Lab. Evaluation trials did \emph{not} use an in-scene chart for the primary path.

\subsection{Core inference (all trials)}

Let $\mathbf{A}$ denote aligned no-flash linear RGB and $\mathbf{B}$ flash linear RGB after enhanced-correlation-coefficient (ECC) registration and skin-mask median-luma exposure matching (flash scaled to match no-flash on a MediaPipe face-mesh skin mask, not global scene scaling). Per-pixel reflectance was
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{R} = \sqrt{\max(\mathbf{A}\odot\mathbf{B},\,\varepsilon)}.
\end{equation}
This geometric mean is a practical flash/no-flash recipe and is \emph{not} the reflectance model of Lu \& Drew (2006). Reflectance was scaled by the participant exposure anchor, converted to CIELAB (D65) via $\mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{aff}}$, and averaged inside a cheek region of interest: intersection of the mesh skin mask and a convex cheek hull, with symmetric 5\% trimmed means on $L^*$, $a^*$, $b^*$ and minimum skin chroma $C_{ab}^*\ge 2$.

\subsection{In-scene Sephora bag chromatic adaptation (\texttt{cat02\_bag})}

On Sephora Bag trials only, we estimated a chromatic-adaptation correction from the in-scene bag before forming $\mathbf{R}$. The white horizontal stripe of a handheld Sephora bag was localized in flash-aligned linear RGB by horizontal stripe-energy scan, refined by two-hand MediaPipe bounding boxes when both hands were visible; optional MobileSAM tightened paper segmentation. Mean linear RGB of the white stripe (centre third of the stripe band) was mapped to XYZ via $\mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{aff}}$.

A CIECAM02 CAT02 transform mapped scene white to Nix Spectro~2 (M2) D65 white/black measurements of the physical bag (\texttt{calibration/sephora\_bag\_nix\_reference.json}). The adaptation was conjugated into camera RGB,
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{P} = \mathbf{M}^{-1}\,\mathbf{T}_{\mathrm{CAT02}}\,\mathbf{M},
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{M}$ is the $3\times3$ linear part of $\mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{aff}}$. When $\mathbf{P}$ was valid, it was applied to \emph{both} no-flash and flash linear RGB; flash was re-warped with the original ECC matrix; skin-mask flash exposure matching was repeated; then $\mathbf{R}$ was computed as above.

\textbf{Trial gating (production).} Bag CAT02 was attempted only when the manifest indicated Sephora Bag (\texttt{condition\_code=BAG}). ColorChecker trials received no in-scene chromatic correction (in-scene CC-based modes increased error on this dark-room cohort). Unknown trial types required hand landmarks in the segmentation path to reject stripe-only false positives on chart whites.

\subsection{Excluded from primary analysis}

Lu \& Drew ambient white-balance paths, SCR-AWB, booth RGB pre-white-balance before reflectance, and FitSkin $L^*$ lightness calibration were not applied on the Pansor primary analysis (cross-session reference already differs in $L^*$). In-scene ColorChecker chromatic correction at inference was rejected by ablation.

\section{Results}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Overview}

We report cheek CIEDE2000 ($\deee$) between Pipeline~4 output and cross-session FitSkin reference (May~20 median cheek Lab per participant, D65). The locked production rule applies \texttt{cat02\_bag} on six Sephora Bag trials and \texttt{none} (reflectance only) on six ColorChecker trials. Primary endpoint on bag trials is mean/median $\deee$ under \texttt{cat02\_bag} versus \texttt{none}. Absolute $\deee$ is not directly comparable to the same-session booth Phase~4 cohort (median $\deee=3.50$, $N=5$); Pansor results should be interpreted as \emph{relative} improvement from in-scene bag chromatic adaptation.

%-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Summary of findings}
%-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Bag CAT02 improves mean agreement on bag trials} (6.50 $\rightarrow$ 5.02 $\deee$; median 6.52 $\rightarrow$ 4.61).
  \item \textbf{Benefit is participant-dependent:} P2 gained $\approx$3 $\deee$ on all bag trials; P1 was flat overall with one failure (P1 Bag T1).
  \item \textbf{CC trials remain uncorrected} (mean 5.39 $\deee$); in-scene CC chromatic modes hurt on this cohort.
  \item \textbf{Trial gating is required:} ungated bag CAT02 on ColorChecker frames causes stripe false-positives and large errors.
  \item \textbf{Cross-session reference inflates absolute $\deee$} vs.\ Phase~4; report Pansor as relative bag-correction gain, not absolute scanner agreement.
\end{enumerate}

%=============================================================================
\section{Discussion points (brief)}
%=============================================================================

\begin{itemize}
  \item Report Pansor primarily as \textbf{relative improvement from bag CAT02} on bag trials ($-1.5$ mean $\deee$), not as absolute same-session scanner agreement.
  \item \textbf{Do not} apply bag CAT02 on ColorChecker frames without trial gating: stripe detection false-positives on chart whites caused large errors when ungated.
  \item Production deployment should log \texttt{bag\_cat02\_status} and treat P1 Bag T1--style failures as first-class outcomes.
  \item Reproducibility: \url{https://github.com/RooneyEmily/Fitskin} (\texttt{flash\_no\_flash\_skin\_lab.py}, \texttt{bag\_chromatic\_correction.py}).
\end{itemize}

\section*{Results paragraph (copy-paste for main paper)}
\small
On the Pansor iPhone cohort ($N=12$ trial pairs; two participants $\times$ Sephora Bag and ColorChecker $\times$ three trials), we compared Pipeline~4 cheek CIELAB to cross-session FitSkin reference (May~20 median cheek Lab). On six Sephora Bag trials, in-scene bag CAT02 chromatic adaptation (\texttt{cat02\_bag}) reduced mean cheek $\deee$ from 6.50 to 5.02 versus chart-free geometric reflectance alone (median 6.52 $\rightarrow$ 4.61; four of six trials improved, 67\% hit rate). Gains were strongest for Participant~2 (mean $\deee$ 7.46 $\rightarrow$ 4.49 on all three bag trials; Participant~1 showed no net improvement). Six ColorChecker trials used reflectance without in-scene chromatic correction (mean $\deee$ 5.39; Participant~1 mean 1.96, Participant~2 mean 8.82). Under the locked production rule (bag $\rightarrow$ \texttt{cat02\_bag}, CC $\rightarrow$ \texttt{none}), the 12-trial mean $\deee$ was 5.21 (median 4.96), versus 5.95 (median 6.52) for reflectance alone on all trials. Absolute $\deee$ exceeded the same-session booth Phase~4 cohort (median 3.50, $N=5$), consistent with cross-session reference and app-exported ProRAW capture; the primary Pansor result is relative improvement from bag CAT02 on bag trials ($-$1.5 mean $\deee$).

\section*{Methods paragraph (copy-paste for main paper)}
\small
Paired iPhone ProRAW (DNG) images were demosaiced to linear camera RGB with embedded camera white balance. The flash frame was ECC-registered to no-flash; flash exposure was scaled to match skin-mask median luma. On Sephora Bag trials, the bag white stripe was segmented in linear RGB (stripe-energy scan plus two-hand MediaPipe refinement); a CAT02 matrix mapped scene white to Nix Spectro~2 D65 bag reference and was applied to both frames before re-warp and reflectance. Per-pixel reflectance was $\mathbf{R}=\sqrt{\mathbf{A}\odot\mathbf{B}}$. ColorChecker trials received no in-scene chromatic correction. Reflectance was converted to cheek CIELAB via offline affine ColorChecker calibration, per-participant exposure anchor, and a MediaPipe cheek ROI (5\% trimmed means). Bag CAT02 was gated to bag trials only (\texttt{condition\_code=BAG}).
