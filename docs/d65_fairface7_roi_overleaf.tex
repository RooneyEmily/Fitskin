\begin{abstract}
We describe a chart-free iPhone ProRAW flash/no-flash pipeline for cheek CIELAB under D65, evaluated against FitSkin Inside Lab on the Pansor-20 indoor cohort ($N=65$ trials, 20~participants).
The \textbf{winning deployment stack}, named \pipename{}, combines (i)~a \emph{frozen} colorimetric path---pre-AWB geometric-mean reflectance, a pooled $3{\times}4$ camera RGB$\to$XYZ affine, and Bradford chromatic adaptation from a fixed 5500\,K Planckian white to D65---with (ii)~a \emph{heuristic} cheek ROI policy whose ethnicity branch is supplied at runtime by FairFace-7 on a landmark face crop (no demographics labels).
Mean CIEDE2000 is $\deee=\num{3.63}$ (median $\num{3.44}$), improving on the frozen trimmed-mean baseline ($\deee=\num{5.55}$) and a production camera-WB affine gate ($\deee\approx\num{5.93}$), while remaining above an oracle that uses true demographics ethnicity for ROI routing ($\deee=\num{3.23}$).
We document rejected alternatives (tone-specific dark matrices, FitSkin-trained Lab correctors, SCR-AWB as the primary illuminant, and blanket remapping of FairFace Latino/Southeast-Asian predictions to Indian ROI rules) and emphasize that FairFace routing is an ROI prior, not part of the colorimetric model.
\end{abstract}

\section{Problem setting and naming}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Goal}
Recover a single cheek CIELAB triple under CIE illuminant D65 from an indoor iPhone flash/no-flash ProRAW pair, without an in-scene ColorChecker at inference, and without FitSkin contact measurements in the color transform.
FitSkin Inside Lab is used \emph{only} as the evaluation target.

\subsection{Pipeline name}
We refer to the recommended deployment configuration as
\begin{center}
  \textbf{\pipename{}}
\end{center}
(``D65'' = evaluation white point; ``FairFace7'' = seven-class race prior; ``ROI'' = specular/shadow-aware cheek sampling).
CLI equivalent:
\begin{verbatim}
python3 scripts/evaluate_pansor20_chartfree_d65.py \
  --scr-mode preawb_cat --fixed-cat-k 5500 \
  --l-sampling fairface7 --emily-tsv
\end{verbatim}

\subsection{What is frozen vs.\ heuristic}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Frozen color path (claimable colorimetry):}
    pre-AWB reflectance $\to$ pooled \texttt{tier3\_affine} $\to$ fixed 5500\,K Bradford CAT$\to$D65 $\to$ trimmed-mean cheek Lab.
    Alone: mean $\deee=\num{5.55}$.
  \item \textbf{Heuristic ROI policy (not a colorimetric model):}
    ethnicity-conditioned cheek $L^*$ / chroma sampling (\texttt{specular\_tone}), with ethnicity predicted by FairFace-7.
    Cohort-tuned thresholds; expected to regress on held-out phones/lighting.
\end{itemize}

%=============================================================================
\section{Cohort, reference, and metrics}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Indoor chart-free Pansor-20 set}
We use only indoor face captures from the Pansor Dataset (Participants~1--20): zip archives containing paired no-flash / flash DNGs and Apple Vision face landmarks.
Excluded stems containing \texttt{bag}, \texttt{outside}, or \texttt{light} (bag / outdoor / lightbox variants).
This yields $N=65$ trials (same indoor chart-free cohort size as the Hybrid D65 Colab walkthrough).
Trials P16\_T1 and P18\_T1 are absent from this set.

\subsection{Demographics and FitSkin reference}
FitSkin Inside Lab $(L^*,a^*,b^*)$ and self-reported ethnicity (Asian, Black, Indian, Iranian, White) come from \texttt{Pansor Dataset Demographics.xlsx}.
Ethnicity is used for oracle ablations and stratified reporting; \textbf{it is not an input to \pipename{} at deployment}.

\subsection{Error metric}
Agreement is CIEDE2000 ($\deee$) between pipeline cheek Lab and FitSkin Inside Lab under D65~\cite{sharma2005ciede2000}.
We report mean and median over trials, and means stratified by demographics ethnicity.

%=============================================================================
\section{Data ingestion}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Demosaic}
Each DNG is demosaiced with \texttt{rawpy} to linear camera RGB:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \texttt{half\_size=True} (evaluation default);
  \item \texttt{gamma=(1,1)}, \texttt{no\_auto\_bright=True}, 16-bit;
  \item per-image scale by the 99.5th percentile of RGB so values are roughly in $[0,1]$.
\end{itemize}
Two white-balance modes are retained for ablations:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item \textbf{Camera WB:} \texttt{use\_camera\_wb=True} (production / older reflectance path);
  \item \textbf{Pre-AWB:} \texttt{use\_camera\_wb=False} (winning color path).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Cheek ROI geometry}
Apple Vision landmark JSON in each zip defines face regions.
We build a cheek mask as the intersection of a face hull (face contour $\cup$ brows) with an eye--mouth vertical band, excluding dilated eyes/brows/lips---matching the zip-landmark path used in prior Hybrid D65 evaluations (not MediaPipe unless \texttt{--roi mediapipe}).

\subsection{Flash exposure match and reflectance}
Let $\mathbf{A}$ be no-flash linear RGB and $\mathbf{B}$ flash linear RGB (resized to $\mathbf{A}$ if needed).
Flash is scaled so median luma on the cheek mask matches no-flash:
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{B}' = s\,\mathbf{B},\qquad
  s = \frac{\mathrm{median}\{\ell(\mathbf{A})\}_{\mathrm{cheek}}}
           {\mathrm{median}\{\ell(\mathbf{B})\}_{\mathrm{cheek}}+\varepsilon},
\end{equation}
with $\ell(\cdot)$ Rec.\,709 luma.
Per-pixel flash/no-flash reflectance is the geometric mean
\begin{equation}
  \Rvec = \sqrt{\max(\mathbf{A}\odot\mathbf{B}',\,\varepsilon)}.
\end{equation}
Pre-AWB frames yield $\Rvec_0$ from $(\mathbf{A}_0,\mathbf{B}_0')$; camera-WB frames yield $\Rvec$.

%=============================================================================
\section{Frozen colorimetric path}
\label{sec:color}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Camera RGB $\to$ XYZ}
Cheek pixels of reflectance are mapped to CIE XYZ (approximate camera space) by the pooled offline affine
\begin{equation}
  \begin{bmatrix} X & Y & Z \end{bmatrix}
  =
  \begin{bmatrix} R & G & B & 1 \end{bmatrix}
  \Maff,
\end{equation}
with $\Maff\in\mathbb{R}^{4\times 3}$ loaded from \texttt{calibration/tier3\_affine}
(\texttt{camera\_rgb\_to\_xyz\_affine.npy} / calibration bundle).
Negative XYZ values are clipped at zero before Lab.

\subsection{Fixed Planckian CAT to D65}
Rather than estimating illuminant per image, we adapt from a fixed daylight-like white.
Let $\mathbf{W}_{5500}$ be CIE XYZ of a $Y{=}1$ Planckian radiator at 5500\,K, and $\mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{D65}}=(0.95047,\,1,\,1.08883)$.
Bradford chromatic adaptation~\cite{lam1985,fairchild2013} maps scene XYZ to D65:
\begin{equation}
  \mathbf{X}_{\mathrm{D65}}
  =
  \mathbf{X}_{\mathrm{cam}}\,
  \mathbf{M}_{\mathrm{Bradford}}(\mathbf{W}_{5500}\!\to\!\mathbf{W}_{\mathrm{D65}})^{\mathsf{T}}.
\end{equation}
CIELAB is then computed with D65 white.
This path corresponds to evaluation mode \texttt{preawb\_cat} with \texttt{--fixed-cat-k 5500}.

\paragraph{Why pre-AWB + fixed CAT.}
Camera WB already adapts toward an assumed white; stacking SCR/CAT on camera-WB reflectance double-adapts and raised mean $\deee$ in ablations (${\sim}7$).
Pre-AWB reflectance plus a single fixed CAT matched the best FitSkin-free color path among illuminant estimators tried (including daylight SCR variants).

\subsection{Default cheek aggregation (no ROI heuristic)}
On chroma-filtered cheek Lab pixels ($C_{ab}^*=\sqrt{a^{*2}+b^{*2}}\ge 2$ when enough pixels remain), we take a symmetric 5\% trimmed mean on each of $L^*,a^*,b^*$.
This is \texttt{--l-sampling off}: mean $\deee=\num{5.55}$ on $N=65$.

%=============================================================================
\section{Rejected and secondary colorimetric attempts}
\label{sec:rejected}
%=============================================================================

The following were evaluated on the same cohort and \textbf{not} used in \pipename{}:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Tone-specific dark affine for Black participants} (\texttt{tier3\_by\_tone/dark}):
    Black mean $\deee$ rose from ${\sim}6.3$ to ${\sim}11.4$ (L* collapse). Rejected.
  \item \textbf{Residual Color Pass-Through / Lab affine trained on FitSkin:}
    Improves in-sample numbers but violates the FitSkin-free generalization constraint for claims. Rejected for deployment claims.
  \item \textbf{Leave-one-out Lab$\to$FitSkin affine:}
    Mean $\deee\approx\num{4.37}$ but is not a generalization result. Rejected.
  \item \textbf{SCR-AWB (Zhou/Kaida-style) as primary illuminant}~\cite{zhou2025scr}:
    Useful as an illuminant research path; with flash SPD in the basis, CCT often locked near 5500\,K and matched pre-AWB+fixed CAT---gain was preprocessing/CAT, not per-image ISSA SCR.
    Daylight SCR variants did not beat $\num{5.55}$ enough to replace the frozen path.
  \item \textbf{MediaPipe cheek ROI} with the same affine:
    Worse than Apple landmark cheeks on this cohort (${\sim}6.4$ vs.\ production landmark baseline).
\end{itemize}

%=============================================================================
\section{Heuristic cheek sampling (\texttt{specular\_tone})}
\label{sec:roi}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Motivation}
Error diagnosis on the frozen path showed \textbf{signed $L^*$ bias by tone}:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
  \item Black: pipeline $L^*$ too high (flash speculars; mean $\Delta L^*\approx +6.4$);
  \item White (underexposed cheeks): $L^*$ too low;
  \item Indian / Asian / Iranian: mixed shadow vs.\ slight over-bright subgroups.
\end{itemize}
These are addressed by changing \emph{which cheek pixels} define Lab---not by changing $\Maff$ or CAT.

\subsection{Oracle policy (demographics ethnicity)}
When true ethnicity is available (ablation only), \texttt{specular\_tone} applies:

\begin{description}[style=nextline,leftmargin=1.2em]
  \item[Black]
    $L^* \leftarrow$ 10th percentile of cheek $L^*$; $a^*,b^*$ from trimmed means
    (reject specular inflation).
  \item[White]
    If mean cheek $L^* < 58$: $L^* \leftarrow$ 70th percentile; else trimmed mean
    (boost underexposed light skin; skip already-bright faces).
  \item[Indian]
    If mean $a^* < 11.5$: drop darkest 20\% of $L^*$ and take $a^*,b^*$ from the higher-chroma half
    (shadow / desaturation);
    else $L^* \leftarrow$ 35th percentile (mild darken for high-$a^*$ cheeks).
  \item[Asian / Iranian (medium)]
    If $a^* < 10$: same shadow+high-chroma branch;
    else if mean $C_{ab}^* < 24$: $L^* \leftarrow$ 70th percentile;
    else trimmed mean.
\end{description}

Oracle (demographics) mean $\deee=\num{3.23}$.
This is an \textbf{in-sample heuristic ceiling}, not a validated colorimetric claim.

\subsection{FitSkin-free routers (before FairFace)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Lab tone/chroma logistic ethnicity classifier}
    (features: $L^*,a^*,b^*,C^*,\mathrm{ITA},L_{p10,50,90}$; labels = demographics ethnicity; never FitSkin Lab).
    Leave-one-subject-out (LOSO): ethnicity accuracy ${\sim}52\%$, mean $\deee\approx\num{3.89}$.
  \item \textbf{Soft accuracy} (same ROI branch despite wrong ethnicity label): ${\sim}75\%$---many Asian/Indian/Iranian confusions are harmless for sampling.
\end{itemize}
Conclusion: race-from-Lab is weak; a face CNN prior is preferable for deployment.

%=============================================================================
\section{FairFace runtime prior}
\label{sec:fairface}
%=============================================================================

\subsection{Model}
We use FairFace ResNet-34~\cite{karkkainen2021fairface} with the 7-class race head
(White, Black, Latino\_Hispanic, East Asian, Southeast Asian, Indian, Middle Eastern),
weights \texttt{res34\_fair\_align\_multi\_7\_20190809.pt}.
The 4-class model was also evaluated; it raised mean $\deee$ to $\num{4.17}$ (Iranian$\to$Indian and some Indian$\to$Black errors) and is \textbf{not} used.

\subsection{Face crop (no dlib)}
From the pre-AWB no-flash frame we form an 8-bit sRGB preview (per-channel percentile stretch + gamma-less display path used elsewhere for detection).
Apple Vision landmarks define a padded axis-aligned face crop, resized to $224{\times}224$ RGB (approximation of FairFace's aligned face chip).

\subsection{Label map to ROI branches}
FairFace labels are mapped to the ethnicity keys expected by \texttt{specular\_tone}:
\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
FairFace-7 & ROI branch key \\
\midrule
Black & Black \\
White & White \\
East Asian, Southeast Asian & Asian \\
Indian & Indian \\
Middle Eastern & Iranian \\
Latino\_Hispanic & Asian \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\paragraph{Recommendation on grouping.}
We \textbf{do not} remap Latino\_Hispanic / Southeast Asian to Indian.
That remap fixes some Indian trials (e.g.\ Utsav) but harms others (e.g.\ Eric, Sanaz) and raises overall mean $\deee$ (e.g.\ $\num{3.63}\to\num{3.84}$).
Asian medium ROI is the safer default for FairFace's ambiguous medium predictions.

\subsection{Deployment algorithm}
\begin{algorithm}[H]
\caption{\pipename{} inference (chart-free)}
\begin{algorithmic}[1]
\Require Flash/no-flash DNGs, Apple landmarks, $\Maff$, FairFace-7 weights
\State Demoise/demosaic both DNGs \textbf{without} camera WB $\to \mathbf{A}_0,\mathbf{B}_0$
\State Build cheek mask from landmarks; exposure-match flash on cheek; $\Rvec_0=\sqrt{\mathbf{A}_0\odot\mathbf{B}_0'}$
\State Preview $\mathbf{A}_0$; crop face; FairFace-7 $\to$ ethnicity key $e$
\State Map cheek $\Rvec_0$ pixels $\xrightarrow{\Maff}$ XYZ $\xrightarrow{\mathrm{CAT}_{5500\to\mathrm{D65}}}$ Lab
\State Aggregate Lab with \texttt{specular\_tone} rules for ethnicity $e$
\State \Return cheek $(L^*,a^*,b^*)$ under D65
\end{algorithmic}
\end{algorithm}

FairFace-7 hard ethnicity accuracy vs.\ demographics is ${\sim}69\%$ on this cohort; Black and White are essentially correct.
Indian is the weakest stratum: FairFace often predicts Latino\_Hispanic or Southeast Asian, which we map to Asian ROI---acceptable for many trials, but costly when the Indian high-$a^*$ ($L_{p35}$) branch was needed (notably Utsav).

\subsection{FairFace-4 vs.\ FairFace-7}
FairFace-4 hard accuracy was higher (${\sim}78\%$) but mean $\deee$ worse ($\num{4.17}$), driven by Iranian$\to$Indian and selected Indian$\to$Black misroutes.
\textbf{FairFace-7 is preferred.}

\section{Limitations and reporting guidance}
%=============================================================================

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{\texttt{specular\_tone} thresholds} ($L$ percentiles, $a^*$/$C^*$ gates, $L^*{<}58$) were tuned on this cohort.
    Treat ROI as a heuristic; quote frozen-path $\deee=\num{5.55}$ for colorimetry-only claims.
  \item \textbf{FairFace} is trained on sRGB web faces; ProRAW preview crops may shift predictions.
    It is a demographic/ROI prior, not a skin-tone instrument.
  \item \textbf{Single phone / indoor set.} Cross-device and outdoor generalization are unproven here.
  \item \textbf{Cross-session FitSkin.} Demographics FitSkin Lab is not same-moment paired with every trial; absolute $\deee$ includes temporal and protocol variance.
  \item \textbf{Ethics.} Race classification in product flows requires careful UX and policy framing; prefer describing FairFace as an appearance prior for sampling, not identity assertion.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Suggested paper wording.}
``We freeze a FitSkin-free colorimetric path (pre-AWB flash/no-flash reflectance, pooled affine, fixed 5500\,K Bradford adaptation to D65; mean $\deee=\num{5.55}$).
Optionally, FairFace-7 on a landmark face crop selects a cheek sampling policy that reduces mean $\deee$ to $\num{3.63}$ without demographics labels at runtime.
The sampling policy is a heuristic ROI router, validated against FitSkin for error reporting only.''
